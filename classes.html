Classes & Curriculum

Everything your sailor will learn on and off the water

What We Teach

Points of Sail

Understanding wind direction relative to the boat — from close-hauled to running downwind. Sailors learn to identify each point of sail and how their boat should behave, building the mental map they'll use every time they're on the water.

Sail Trim Basics

How to adjust sails for maximum power and control, including reading telltales to know whether sails are correctly trimmed. Sailors practice pulling in and easing the mainsheet and jib to feel how the boat accelerates and points.

Tacking & Jibing

The two fundamental maneuvers for changing direction — tacking through the wind and jibing away from it. Covers crew coordination, timing, and how to avoid getting stuck "in irons" (head-to-wind with no steerage). Common mistakes are discussed and drilled on the water.

Right of Way / Rules of the Road

Who yields to whom when boats meet: port vs. starboard tack, windward vs. leeward boat, and overtaking rules. Understanding these basics keeps everyone safe and is the foundation for any future racing or passage-making.

Safety & Capsize Recovery

Life jacket use and fit, man-overboard procedures, and how to read weather conditions before heading out. Sailors also practice righting a capsized dinghy so that tipping over becomes a manageable learning moment, not a scary emergency.
