Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need sailing experience?

Not at all. The program is built for beginners — we start from the very basics, so you can show up knowing nothing about sailing and still keep up.

Am I the right age?

The program is open to sailors ages 8–14. If you're close to those ages, reach out and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

What should I bring?

We'll send you a full list of what to wear and bring before the program starts. Life jackets are provided — just show up ready to have fun on the water.

Have another question?

Send us a message at pnwaysc@gmail.com or call 425-999-6541 and we'll be happy to help.
