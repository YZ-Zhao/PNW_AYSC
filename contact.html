Frequently Asked Questions

Does my child need sailing experience?

No. Our program is designed to welcome young sailors with a range of experience, including complete beginners. We start from the very basics.

What ages can participate?

Our youth program is designed for sailors ages 8–13. If your child is close to these ages, feel free to reach out and we'll do our best to accommodate.

What should my child bring?

We'll provide families with a complete list of recommended clothing and personal items before the program begins. Life jackets are provided.

Have another question?

Send us a message at pnwaysc@gmail.com or call 425-999-6541 and we'll be happy to help.
