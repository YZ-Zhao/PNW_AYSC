Welcome to PNW AYSC

Our Mission

Our mission at PNW Youth Sailing Club is to create a welcoming and inclusive community where young sailors ages 8–13 can safely explore the sport of sailing while building confidence, teamwork, and lifelong skills. We aim to inspire personal growth and a lasting appreciation for the water, the outdoors, and the values of respect, responsibility, and perseverance.
