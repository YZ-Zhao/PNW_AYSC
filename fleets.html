Our Fleets

The boats and facilities that power our program

Full fleet details are coming soon. Below is a preview of the types of boats and facilities we use — stay tuned for confirmed locations and availability.

Optimist Dinghy

The Optimist is the world's most widely sailed youth dinghy, designed specifically for sailors ages 7–15. Its stable, forgiving hull makes it perfect for beginners learning the fundamentals of sail trim, tacking, and boat control in a safe and manageable platform.

📍 Kirkland Marina Park, Kirkland, WA 98033

Club 420

The Club 420 is a double-handed dinghy used throughout North America for youth team racing and intermediate development. Sailors who have mastered the basics graduate to the C420, learning crew coordination, spinnaker handling, and advanced racing tactics.

📍 Meydenbauer Bay Park, Bellevue, WA 98004
